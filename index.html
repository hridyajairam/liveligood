A Digital Common for Social Enterprises

Who we are
Liveligood

Liveligood is India's first verified directory and marketplace exclusively for social enterprises. We are a digital commons, an open, shared space where enterprises rooted in social and environmental mission can be discovered, trusted, and supported by buyers who care about impact.

We work at the intersection of commerce and conscience, making it easy for individuals, corporations, and institutions to buy from enterprises that are creating livelihoods, conserving water, reducing carbon, and uplifting communities across India.

Our Vision
An India where social enterprises thrive as the backbone of a regenerative economy, where buying well is the norm, not the exception.
Our Mission
To build a trusted, verified marketplace that connects purpose-led social enterprises with conscious buyers, creating measurable social and environmental impact at scale.
Why we exist
The System Gaps We Are Solving

India has over 3 lakh social enterprises producing extraordinary goods with verified social and environmental impact. Yet they remain invisible to buyers who would choose them. The gap is not demand. It is discovery, trust, and access. Here is why:

01
Unequal Market Conditions
Current market structures reward businesses that maximise profit and scale rapidly. Enterprises that prioritise social impact, environmental sustainability, or community wellbeing compete on unequal terms. Their prices reflect fair wages and ethical sourcing, while businesses with greater financial resources dominate market visibility. Consumer demand for ethical alternatives remains constrained not by lack of interest, but by lack of access and visibility.
02
Barriers to Market Visibility and Access
Marketing and digital platforms determine which products reach public attention, and access depends on advertising budgets and algorithmic optimisation rather than social value. Social media algorithms amplify businesses with greater financial power while emerging ethical enterprises struggle to reach audiences. As a result, consumers rarely encounter locally rooted and impact-driven alternatives, reinforcing a cycle where visibility follows wealth rather than value.
03
Demand-Side Gaps in Livelihood Ecosystems
Public investments in livelihoods largely focus on the supply side: skills training, finance, and incubation. While these create thousands of enterprises, comparatively little is directed towards strengthening market demand for their products. Without sustained access to customers, many enterprises struggle to survive beyond initial support. Evidence from programmes like Kudumbashree shows that enterprise creation alone does not guarantee viability. Market access remains critical yet under-addressed.
The Solution
A 5-Step Systemic Transformation

Liveligood addresses these gaps not as a transactional marketplace, but as a systemic intervention. Our approach moves in five stages:

1
Map
Locate and document emerging and small social enterprises, cooperatives, and ethical businesses across India.
2
Verify
Screen entities to ensure they are registered, use clean processes, focus on people and planet, and reinvest into their purpose.
3
Publish
Launch a social enterprise directory with impact data, team profiles, purchasing links, and contact channels.
4
Advocate
Build widespread market demand through social media storytelling, community events, and direct action programs.
5
Integrate
Embed verified social enterprises into public procurement, corporate supply chains, and institutional purchasing systems.
For Social Enterprises
Helps small, isolated businesses join a visible market network and builds buyer trust through clear, honest verification. Prepares them to meet official corporate or government procurement requirements while boosting their reach through shared storytelling and direct action programs.
For Customers
Allows everyday shoppers to easily find people and planet-friendly enterprises, browse product ranges, and connect directly to honest local businesses. Gives them a direct way to buy from and support the ethical causes they believe in.
For Communities and Planet
Strengthens local economies by helping community enterprises sustain livelihoods, retain wealth locally, and scale solutions that respond to local needs. Creates market demand for environmentally responsible businesses, making sustainable production more commercially viable and accelerating the transition towards regenerative economic practices.
Our Process
Verification Framework

Every enterprise on Liveligood goes through a rigorous 5-step verification process before being listed. This is how we ensure our Verified badge means something.

1
Application and Documentation
Enterprise submits registration proof (Section 8, FPO, Producer Company, Trust, etc.), founders profiles, and mission statement.
2
Social Impact Audit
We review livelihoods created, beneficiary demographics, community programs, fair wage practices, environmental policy, and on-ground evidence of social mission.
3
SROI Methodology Review
Our team verifies the Social Return on Investment methodology: inputs, outputs, outcomes, and impact proxies.
4
Product Quality and Compliance
Products are reviewed for quality standards, labelling compliance, and alignment with the enterprise's stated mission.
5
Annual Re-Verification
Listed enterprises undergo annual impact reporting and periodic re-verification to maintain the Liveligood Verified badge.
Methodology
How We Calculate SROI

Social Return on Investment (SROI) measures the social, environmental, and economic value created per rupee spent. For every product on Liveligood, we show verified SROI data from the enterprise.

Example: Rs 200 Tribal Soap

Livelihood income to artisanRs 24 per soap
Water footprint saved vs chemical soap8L per bar
Avoided synthetic chemical harm18g
Forest biodiversity value from herb harvestRs 32 per unit
Total Social Value CreatedRs 3.2 per Rs 1 spent - SROI 3.2x

SROI values shown are estimates based on enterprise-reported data. For bulk orders above Rs 1 lakh, we provide detailed audited SROI reports.
