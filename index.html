🌱 About Liveligood

A Digital Commons
for Social Enterprises

A commons is a shared resource maintained by a community for the benefit of all. Liveligood is that — for social enterprises in India. Open to list on. Free to browse. Built on transparency.

Who We Are
Not a marketplace.
A digital commons.

Liveligood is a verified directory and discovery platform for social enterprises in India. We do not take commissions on sales. We do not charge listing fees. We are a community-built platform that makes social enterprises visible, their impact verifiable, and their products discoverable.

We believe that if people could see the full value of what they buy — not just the price tag, but the livelihoods created, the water saved, the carbon not emitted — they would choose differently. Liveligood is that window.

Every enterprise on this platform has been through our verification process. Every impact number has been reviewed. Every product links directly to its producer — no middlemen, no markup, no mystery.

🔍
Radical Transparency
Every SROI claim is verified. Every registration is checked. Numbers are backed by data, not stories.
🤝
Direct Connection
We never take a cut of product sales. Buyers go directly to producer websites. We exist to build trust — not profit from it.
🌱
Community Ownership
Liveligood is built with and for social enterprises. Their needs define our roadmap.
Why We Exist
India's social enterprises
are invisible.

India has over 2 million social enterprises. Most of them are invisible to mainstream buyers. Their products create real, measurable change — livelihoods for tribal artisans, clean water for rural families, carbon sequestration for a warming planet. But this value is hidden behind a price tag that doesn't tell the full story.

Meanwhile, conscious buyers — individuals, CSR teams, procurement officers — have no reliable way to find verified social enterprises or know if the "impact" claims on a product are real.

Liveligood is the bridge. A place where impact is calculated, verified, and made visible — before the purchase, not after.

The problems we solve
❌
Invisible enterprises
Social enterprises lack digital presence and are not discoverable by buyers who'd choose them.
❌
Unverified impact claims
Greenwashing is rampant. Buyers can't distinguish genuine impact from marketing.
❌
CSR spend misdirected
Corporates want to spend CSR budgets on impact — but verified options are hard to find at scale.

Verification Framework
We verify.
Here's exactly how.

Getting listed on Liveligood is not automatic. Every enterprise goes through our 5-step verification framework. We ask hard questions — kindly. Trust is built through process, not promises.

Average time from application to listing: 10–14 working days

1
Document Review
Submit your registration certificate, GSTIN (if applicable), and founding documents. We verify your legal status and registration type.
2
Mission Alignment
We assess whether your social or environmental mission is central to how you operate — not peripheral. We look at ownership structure, beneficiary model, and surplus usage.
3
Impact Data Submission
You fill our standardised impact template. We work with you to identify the right metrics. You don't need sophisticated monitoring systems; we help you build what you need.
4
Verification Call
A 45-minute call with our impact team to walk through your data, story, and evidence.
5
Listing & Annual Audit
You're listed — free, forever. We re-verify annually to keep your data current and credible.
SROI Methodology
How we calculate
social return.

SROI (Social Return on Investment) measures the social, environmental, and economic value created per rupee spent. On Liveligood, we calculate SROI per product unit — so you see the exact impact of your individual purchase.
