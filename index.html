📐 SROI Methodology

How We Calculate Social Return on Investment

SROI measures the social, environmental, and economic value created per rupee of investment. On Liveligood, we calculate SROI per product unit — so you see the exact impact of your individual purchase.

1
Enterprise Submits Impact Data
Enterprises fill our verified impact template tracking inputs (cost, materials, people) and outputs (units sold, livelihoods, environmental savings) quarterly.
2
Third-Party Verification
Our network of certified SROI practitioners reviews the data and assigns verified impact values per product unit.
3
Per-Unit SROI Calculation
Cumulative impact is divided by units sold to give a per-purchase impact estimate — displayed as SROI chips on every product card.
4
Annual Impact Report
Every enterprise publishes an annual impact report. Bulk buyers receive a customised SROI report with every order.
